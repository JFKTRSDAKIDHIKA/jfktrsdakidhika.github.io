My research lies at the intersection of computer architecture, VLSI, and electronic design automation (EDA), with a current focus on **3D-integrated compute-near-memory architectures**.

I am particularly interested in improving DNN execution efficiency through **fusion-aware scheduling and optimization**. My recent work, **FADiff**, explores a differentiable optimization framework for this direction.
